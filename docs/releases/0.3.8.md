### What's new

- Search results now show their position in the ranking. Image matches also display signed CLIP cosine similarity, including in combined searches; similarity is not a probability.
- Images open directly over the search page and conversation context. Close them with Escape, the close button, or a click on the background without losing the query or results.
- Close buttons for application and dialog settings now stay outside the scrolling panel and remain accessible on desktop and narrow screens.
- Existing models, CPU/GPU embeddings, and indexing progress remain compatible. Updating does not require reindexing or model downloads.
