### What's new

- **Omit an entire noise family.** One button selects every observed variant, including variants on pages you have not opened. Individual variants can still be changed back to Keep.
- **Keep control before applying.** Bulk selection updates the local draft. Save and preview the rules before applying them; protected occurrences and the original archive are preserved.
- **Reliable bulk selection.** Interrupted requests, changed drafts and project limits leave your existing choices intact.

Existing archives, models and indexing progress are preserved. Installing the update does not rebuild indexes.
